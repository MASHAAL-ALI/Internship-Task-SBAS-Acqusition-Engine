Accumulator: 
1. Design a module that will accumulate one 4-bit signed input at frequency of 16.368MHz for every 1ms and output the 16-bit accumulated data.
You have to accumulate data for 1ms only then reset it to zero. For 1ms, the 16 bit data register will not overflow. 

From testbench, give 1ms interrupt to the module. At every posedge of 1ms, the output variable 16 bit ACCUMDATA gives output.
